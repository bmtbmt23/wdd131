About Brazil

Brazil is located in South America. The country has the most biodiverse environments including the Amazon and the Pantanal. Brazilian are extremely friendly, relaxed and welcoming to foreigners. Brazil is the only country to have won the FIFA World Cup five times. There are 24 temples across the country.
